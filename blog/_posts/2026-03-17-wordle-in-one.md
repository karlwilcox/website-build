If you have a chosen start word for Wordle, and you use it consistently, sooner
or later it is likely that your word will match the Wordle word, and today is
that day for me!

The word was chosen to be easily memorable (I always think of the song of that
name from the Jethro Tull album "The Broadsword and the Beast" that I was
really into in my teens...) and helps eliminate or match a good range of the
less common letters (so no 'E's or 'T's as that doesn't really help).

Of course this might be my lucky day but if I want to have a chance of ever
doing this again then I need a new starter word!
