Another show from the talented pen of Mackenzie Crook. Inevitably this will be
compared to [_Detectorists_](/reviews/tvshows/titles/#toD)
so I’ll jump on the bandwagon here too. We’ve had a bit of
a family split here, with me preferring _Detectorists_ and everyone else for
_Small Prophets_. This might be because I’m a history/archaeology buff while the
rest of the household are a bit more spiritual.

That’s not to say anything bad about _Small Prophets_, every character was
clearly drawn, even if some were a bit caricatured. I just personally felt I
had more in common with the DMDC than the workforce of Tods etc.

Pearce Quigley was the stand out star here, ably abetted by Lauren Patel and
Michael Palin (a relative newcomer and an old hand that it was a real pleasure
to see acting again).

There were some really funny moments, and some surprisingly moving ones, all
held together by the deadpan seriousness of Quigley, but backed with real
warmth and caring.

A neat ending that brings everything to a head and resolves much but leaves
enough for that “To be continued” tease to be a gem to look forward to.

Although it does seem Mackenzie wants to get some air miles since there is a
clear set up for the next season to visit both Canada and Australia!

Good luck with season two for everyone…
