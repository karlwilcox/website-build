I don't recall much of the original TV series from my childhood
days, other than I didn't really like it much, and I haven't read
the books so the level of peril and heartache came as something
of a surprise! (I'm sure the 1970's show was really anodyne...)

Lost dogs, lost horses, scary native americans, out of control wild fires,
family schisms and truama, a scheming fat controller and a local loose-cannon
drunk who looked uncannily like Captain Haddock from the Tintin books - it was
all there!

Fortunately there was also a huge amount to like here. Good casting of the
main family who all played really well, plenty of warmth and loving moments
and some real practical issues to be dealt with. There was no sugar
coating of this, I expect it follows the actual books more closely
and would perhaps like to add some of them to my reading list.

I understand this has already been renewed for more seasons and
long may it continue - this season had a real bittersweet ending
(I was expecting a deus-ex-machina moment that never arrived) so
I'm genuinely looking forward to the next season and anxious to
see how they all get on.
