Rintaro talked about Saint-Exupéry in
[The Cat Who Saved Books](/home/reviews/books/cat-who-saved-books/) so
I thought I had better read some to broaden my mind!

Memories of school and stories being read out to us in class made me
think I had already heard "The Little Prince" but ChatGPT tells me that
what I was actually thinking of was Oscar Wilde's "The Happy Prince" so
this story was really new to me.

And what a delight it is. The meditations on the differences between
children and adults made me laugh out loud and the meditative ending
and reflections on friendship and meaning were surprisingly moving.

I don't need to tell the story here, it is very short, very readable
and something that you should look at yourself, I'm sure everyone
will get something out of reading this book, and it may well be
a different something for each person. I had a sense of calm,
right and connection to the people I care about (those that
have "tamed" me, and I them).

An absolute delight.
