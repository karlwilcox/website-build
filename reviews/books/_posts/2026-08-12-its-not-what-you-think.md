Nicely twisty – it’s not what you think about pretty much everything in this
book, there’s really only two main characters who stay consistently, so even if
you spot one of the twists coming don’t worry, there will be others that are
still on their way!

Sadly the story has an undercurrent of genuine nastiness – it may well be true
to life especially with the interference that we see fomented today but to be
honest I read a lot of fiction for escapism and this brings the real world a
little too close for me.

The story itself starts with a rather nasty murder and gets quite dark in
places. It is told from multiple points of view and jumps around in time as
well but is well structured enough to follow easily. I don’t think that the
inclusion of the murder victim’s point of view really worked – we already have
almost everything that was covered and it just felt a little Jamie was very
much past tense for all concerned and introducing his POV only once and midway
through didn’t really work for me.

So if you like twisty and twisted contemporary thrillers with quite an exciting
ending but a little too realistic and nasty for me to enjoy completely.

Plot summary – A mixed race couple become involved with a far-right
organisation whose tentacles extend into the police force, leading to events
that are not all what they seem.
