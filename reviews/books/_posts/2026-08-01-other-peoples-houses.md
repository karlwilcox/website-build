A female welsh detective and her romantic partner over the border in the
Chesire constabulary investigate their own particular crimes (a murder at an
outdoor pursuit centre and a series of burglaries from posh houses
respectively) and we also get transcripts of a true crime / cold case podcast
interspersed - quite a nice touch. Before long all of these cases are tangled
together and things get Mackintosh trade-mark twisty.

Quite a few people here are drawn as rather unpleasant and unlikeable
characters: estate agents, rich people, ambitious constables, the partner's
ex-wife. What I think that the author did need to do however was give us
an actual likeable character to relate to! Both main characters didn't come
across very well, at least in the first three-quartrers of the book, although
events started to move quickly in the final part and it did eventually get
exciting and I did finally start to root for the heroes - although I think
there was some bad, but perhaps understandable, decision making by the partner
that unnecessarily triggered the big scene at the end. Although to be fair
I'm not sure that I would have done any different in his situation.

Had I not been determined to get to the end of this to keep up my reading
stats I might have abandoned this as I didn't really like anybody but it
turned out well in the end and I did enjoy it overall.
