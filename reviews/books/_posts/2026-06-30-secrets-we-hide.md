I pre-ordered this audiobook as soon as I had finished the first of the North
Falls series and listened to it soon after its release.

A gripping story, massively enhanced by the superb performance of the narrator
Kathleen Early who puts on some great accents and a huge amount of passion that
really drags you into the story (although I was sometimes “taken out of it”
every time one of the cops puts on their “doody” vest).

A murder mystery that appears to be a domestic matter when an abused wife seems
to be on the verge of leaving, this turns out to have roots deep in the past.
During the investigation we also learn more of the history of Emmy’s sister
Jude and her childhood (as Martha) and it turns out that the secrets of the
title are not just those of the bad guys.

A completely convincing tale that really puts you inside the claustrophobia of
a town run by the many interlinked descendants of the founding family, but
also showing the opportunities that this networking allows for.

The story takes place over just a few hot days of summer and the pace really
never lets up. This one is more of a two-hander with Emmy and Jude pushing each
other to move forward on the case, while at the same time running a campaign
for re-election as sheriff against the conservatism and misogyny of the police
department.

Another great read from Karin, further improved by Kathleen in the audiobook
version. Well done to both.
