Sticking to the same formula as the first volume this is another delightful
trip to the Funiculi Funicula coffee shop with its unchanging decor and
strange rules for time travel.

The construction of the stories is always clever, especially given the
restrictions imposed by those rules, although perhaps inevitably there
is are a lot of tragic deaths involved. The nice thing is that every
time travel journey improves the happiness of someone and sometimes
both parties come out better for it. I also like the way that the
journeys are all independent, carried out for their own reasons but
that the story as a whole advances as we learn more of the lives of
the coffee shop staff.

I did sometimes struggle with the names (even Miki gets confused them...)
but the helpful chart at the front of the book proved invaluable - every
book should have one!

We've now got someone new in *the* chair so it will be interesting to
see whether we get their back story in the next volume.

A lovely series, thoughtful and charming, with nice pastel colour choices on
the paperbacks.
